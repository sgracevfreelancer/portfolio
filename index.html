About

Design shaped by real business problems.

My work has ranged from wine e-commerce content to community campaigns for local government, but the goal is always the same: design that gets results.

I've spent the last several years designing A+ content, product listing graphics, and social media campaigns for e-commerce brands, most recently in the wine industry, where I handled product photography, banners, and campaign assets from start to finish.

Before that, I wore a lot of hats: virtual assistant, technical support, and even a college IT instructor. That mix taught me to move fast, learn new tools quickly, and design with the end user always in mind, whether that's a shopper, a customer, or a community member.

Today I focus on graphic design and visual content: branding, packaging, digital banners, and short-form video, built with the Adobe Suite, Canva, and AI-assisted workflows.
