Case Study

Martellotto Wines -
2023 Label Design

Four proprietary reds, one Napa Valley family. I designed the label identity for all four 2023 releases, each one built to speak to a different kind of collector while still reading as one house. All four went into production and have been on shelves since last year.

CLIENT - Martellotto Winery ROLE - Label & Packaging Design YEAR - 2024–2025 STATUS - In Production, Shipping Now

The brief. Napa Cabernet doesn't sell on liquid alone. At $60 to $150 a bottle, the label has to earn its spot on a shelf next to names like Shafer and Stag's Leap Wine Cellars. Martellotto asked for four standalone label identities for their 2023 vintage, each aimed at a different kind of buyer, but all clearly part of the same family.

The approach. Rather than one template with swapped colors, each label was built around the personality of the wine inside it: an everyday-luxury red blend, a bold Pinot Noir with real attitude, an accessible entry-point Cabernet, and a reserve-tier Cabernet from Stags Leap District. Every draft went through winery review before approval and print.
